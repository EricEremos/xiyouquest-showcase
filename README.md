## Give the next attempt a reason.

Pronunciation practice asks you to repeat yourself. XiYouQuest places that repetition inside an adventure inspired by *Journey to the West*, with a story to follow and companions to discover.

The current learning hub brings together a story campaign, pronunciation and speaking practice, mock practice, an AI learning path and companion conversations. Choose a focused exercise or return to the journey from the same starting point.

## See where the journey leads.

<p align="center"><img src="assets/journey-map-20261022.png" alt="XiYouQuest story map with seven stages across a mountain landscape: the prologue is available and later stages are visibly locked" width="100%"></p>

The seven-stage map begins with a prologue and continues through rivers, mountains and forests towards the Western Palace. Later stages are visibly locked in this captured starting state, making the next destination part of the experience.

## Practise with a familiar face.

<p align="center"><img src="assets/companions-20261022.png" alt="Current companion selection: Sun Wukong is available, while Tang Sanzang, Sha Wujing and Zhu Bajie show their later-stage unlock requirements" width="100%"></p>

Sun Wukong is available at the start. The companion screen introduces Tang Sanzang, Sha Wujing and Zhu Bajie with their stage requirements, connecting conversation practice to the larger story.

For more focused work, the practice catalogue offers pronunciation, reading and prompted speaking alongside supplementary drills. The site also provides mock practice; it does not issue an official PSC result or proficiency certification.

## My contribution

**Winner · HKUST GenAI Hackathon 2026 (team project).**

XiYouQuest is a team project. I contribute to planning, interface work, backend implementation and testing.

One small detail captures the work well: learners should be able to hear the reference and replay their own attempt without the two interfering. [This contribution](https://github.com/baduru11/XiYouQuest-RPG-study-web/commit/2469cefd609cba3154a87a52138dc824160a5ea4) connects that learning need with playback behaviour, recording controls and regression tests.

It is the same approach I bring to mentoring: start with a useful idea, turn it into a working experience, then inspect the details that determine whether it helps. We can work through those decisions together—from the initial question to an interface you can see and test.
